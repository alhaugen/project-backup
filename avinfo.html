About the Property

Located in the peaceful rural village of Gálipe, Crete, Amplades Villas consists of three traditional stone villas that have been lovingly renovated. The estate beautifully blends historical elements—such as century-old architectural details—with modern, upscale comfort for a tranquil island getaway.

Amplades One-Bedroom Villa

Best For: Couples, honeymooners, or solo travelers seeking an exclusive, romantic retreat.

📐 Layout & Amenities

  • Capacity Limits: Strictly limited to 2 Adults Maximum (No children or infants permitted).
  • Bedding configuration: 1 Plush King-size master bed.
  • Living Space: Open-plan historic stone architecture, cozy lounge area, and a fully equipped kitchenette.
  • Outdoor Space: Private sun-drenched terrace facing local flora.

Amplades Two-Bedroom Villa

Best For: Families or small groups looking for a spacious suite with a blend of country charm and privacy.

📐 Layout & Amenities
