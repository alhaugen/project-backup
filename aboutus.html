Welcome to Crete GR Travel

Our company started with just a few villas, the Amplades Villas, and has grown into a full-service travel experience provider for the Greek Island of Crete. We are passionate about sharing the authentic Cretan lifestyle with our guests, from the rich history and culture to the breathtaking landscapes and culinary delights.

Amplades stands for "sisters" in the traditional Cretan dialect. Why did we name our properties Amplades? We are three sisters that came together to save and preserve an old historic property, and at the same time offer a traditional, authentic Cretan experience to our children, friends, and guests from all over the world.

It is a place where you can make unforgettable memories right here, in our mythical place of birth, the majestic Greek Island of Crete.

🏡 Our Original Location

We created three unique, autonomous suites, hidden away like a gem in the small mountainous village of Galipe. Located only 20 minutes from the airport of Heraklion, they are just far enough from the loud touristy areas so that you are able to taste the real Cretan life that many never get to experience—but still close enough to main attractions and captivating beaches.

📐 Historical Glimpse & Modern Comforts

Our traditional villas give you a glimpse into the noble past of Cretan Character, while offering all the comforts of today’s world. Each suite can comfortably sleep up to 4 people and is fully equipped with:

  • 📡 Free High-Speed Wi-Fi & Smart TV
  • ❄️ Full Air-Conditioning
  • ☕ Cozy Patio to enjoy a breath of fresh mountain air that clears mind and soul

✨ The Spirit of Cretan Philoxenia

Our Greek culture, especially here in Crete, is all about the traditions of Hospitality—or Philoxenia, as we say here! We can’t wait for you to experience the warmth of Cretan Philoxenia while you stay at one of our charming traditional suites that will take you back to another era!!
